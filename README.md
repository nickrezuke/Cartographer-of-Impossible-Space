## Project Overview
During Summer 2026, a team consisting of [Nick Rezuke](https://github.com/nickrezuke), [Anthony Amaral](https://github.com/Antdominator), [Ryan Pereira](https://github.com/ryanpereira849), and [Shaun Jamele](https://github.com/shaunj0116-fusion26) designed a Unity game. We created a Sokoban-style puzzle game in which the player controls an ant navigating a 3D cube-like labyrinth. In this game, the player pushes blocks around to open paths that let them move forward throughout the levels, and each side of this cube represents a different room to explore. What makes this game stand out from other Sokoban games is its use of 2D and 3D elements to represent boxes connected to other rooms in the later levels. This means the player will need to be mindful of how their actions (specifically regarding box placement) in one room impact another room on the opposite side of the cube. The objective of this game is to navigate all six sides/levels before reaching the goal and exiting the labyrinth.
